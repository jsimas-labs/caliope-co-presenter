Presenting without touching the keyboard

An AI co-presenter that listens, talks to the audience and runs the demo live.

Opening. The presenter introduces the idea: today they will not touch the keyboard. The assistant is called Calíope and, when called, introduces herself in one sentence: she listens, changes slides and runs the demo like a user.
The problem

Presenting and running the demo at the same time splits attention

  • Switching windows breaks the story's rhythm
  • Typing under pressure means live mistakes
  • The presenter stops looking at the audience
A pain every technical presenter knows: the demo steals attention. Three symptoms: switching windows, live typos and losing contact with the audience.
How it works

Listens, decides, acts and speaks

01ListensThe voice goes to a real-time voice model, which only answers when it hears her name.
02DecidesThe model picks a tool: change slide, open page, click, fill.
03ActsPlaywright moves the cursor and types in the browser, like a real user.
04SpeaksHer voice enters Meet as a second microphone.
Architecture in four steps. Listens: continuous audio to the real-time voice API, with the assistant's name working as the wake word. Decides: function calling. Acts: Playwright drives the stage window, with a visible cursor. Speaks: the voice goes out through a virtual audio device that Meet uses as a microphone.
Live demo

Calíope will open an account at the Demo Bank, like a customer would

Nobody touches the keyboard: she fills in the form, accepts the terms and submits.

ask: "Calíope, open an account for Maria Oliveira"
Demo. Calíope opens the Demo Bank, fills in name, email, income and product, accepts the terms and clicks Open account. Then she reads the reference number to the audience. The Demo Bank is a test page with no real data.
